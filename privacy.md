# TraceAR Privacy Policy

**Draft — pending final review and publication.**

Last updated: 27 September 2026

## Who is responsible

TraceAR is a mixed-reality image tracing application for Meta Quest, published under the developer name Toti Studio. The person or legal entity responsible for its data processing is **Abdulbari Abdlbari, Sweden**. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact **tracearcontact@gmail.com**.

## Data the app processes

- **Images you choose:** TraceAR reads images you explicitly select through the device's file picker and stores imported copies in its local app storage. It uses these images to display tracing references. TraceAR does not upload your images to a developer-operated server.
- **Local preferences and recent images:** The app stores your measurement-unit preference and references to recently selected images on your device to support the library and your preferred display units.
- **Headset and controller tracking:** The app uses device-provided positions, orientations and controller inputs during use to place and manipulate images and menus. TraceAR does not maintain a tracking-history database or transmit tracking data to Toti Studio.
- **Passthrough:** Meta Quest provides the view of your surroundings used for mixed reality. TraceAR does not record or upload passthrough video to Toti Studio.
- **Meta account and purchases:** Through the Meta Platform SDK, the app temporarily processes your app-scoped Meta user ID, app entitlement and purchase information to check access to the app, display the localized Pro price, verify the Pro upgrade and restore purchases. The user ID is handled in memory, not saved in an app-created account database. Meta's durable purchase cache may provide purchase ownership when online verification is unavailable.

TraceAR does not operate its own user-account service or developer-hosted image storage. It does not include advertising or developer-operated behavioral analytics. We do not sell personal information. Payment is handled by Meta; TraceAR does not receive your payment-card details.

## Who receives data

Images and local preferences are processed on your device. Toti Studio does not have remote access to that app storage. Meta processes account, entitlement and transaction information through its platform services under its own privacy policies. See [Meta's Privacy Policy](https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/).

If you choose to contact us, we receive the contact details and information you provide so that we can respond. Please do not send payment-card details, passwords or unnecessary personal information. The optional Discord link opens an external service: information you choose to share there is subject to Discord's policies and the visibility of the channel you use.

Device-level services, including any backup you enable, are governed by the platform's settings and policies. This policy does not override Meta's handling of its own account or transaction records.

## Retention and deletion

Imported copies, recent-image references and preferences remain in local app storage until that data is removed. Transient tracking and account-check data are used during app operation rather than stored as a history by TraceAR. Meta controls retention of its purchase records and SDK-managed caches.

To remove TraceAR's local imported copies and preferences, clear its app data using the device's app-management tools where available, or uninstall the app. Original image files outside TraceAR's app storage are not removed by uninstalling TraceAR; delete those separately through your device's file manager if desired. Check your Meta cloud-backup settings if you also want to remove or prevent restoration of a device backup.

**All users may request deletion of any personal information held by Toti Studio by emailing tracearcontact@gmail.com with the subject “TraceAR data deletion”.** We will explain what information, if any, we hold and handle your request in accordance with applicable law. We cannot remotely erase local headset files or delete Meta's own account and purchase records. For those records, use Meta's privacy controls or contact Meta directly. Removing local app data does not cancel or refund a Meta purchase.

If you contact us, we retain correspondence only as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request its deletion through the same contact address.

## Your choices and rights

You choose which images to import and whether to purchase Pro or open external links. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal information, or restrict or object to processing. Contact us at the address above to exercise applicable rights. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

## Changes

We will update this page when our data practices change and revise the date above. This policy applies to TraceAR, including builds previously named TraceNest.
